What is CGPA?

CGPA stands for Cumulative Grade Point Average. It is commonly used by schools, colleges and universities to represent a student's academic performance over multiple subjects or semesters.

CGPA is usually represented on a scale such as 10 points, although the grading scale can vary depending on the educational institution.

How to Calculate CGPA

If all semesters have equal weight, CGPA can be calculated by adding the semester grade points and dividing the total by the number of semesters.

CGPA = Sum of Grade Points ÷ Number of Semesters

Example

Suppose your semester grade points are:

8.0, 8.5, 9.0 and 8.5

The CGPA would be:

(8.0 + 8.5 + 9.0 + 8.5) ÷ 4 = 8.50

CGPA to Percentage

The method for converting CGPA to percentage depends on the grading system and rules used by your school, college or university.

Different institutions can use different conversion formulas, so students should check their official academic guidelines before using a CGPA-to-percentage conversion.
