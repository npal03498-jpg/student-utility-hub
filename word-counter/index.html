What is a Word Counter?

A word counter is an online tool that counts the number of words in a piece of text. It can also provide useful statistics such as character count, sentence count, paragraph count and estimated reading time.

How Does a Word Counter Work?

The tool analyzes the text you enter and calculates different statistics automatically. Words are counted based on groups of characters separated by whitespace.

Why Use a Word Counter?

A word counter can be useful for students, writers and anyone who needs to meet a specific word limit.

  • Check assignment and essay word limits.
  • Count words in articles and blog posts.
  • Check character limits for applications.
  • Estimate how long a text may take to read.
  • Quickly check the length of written content.

Word Counter for Students

Students often need to write assignments, essays, reports and applications with specific word requirements. A word counter makes it easy to check whether your writing is within the required limit.

Word Count vs Character Count

Word count measures the number of words in a text, while character count measures individual characters. Character count can include spaces, punctuation and other characters.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I count words in a text?

Paste or type your text into the text box above. The word count is calculated automatically.

Does the word counter count characters?

Yes. StudentHub's word counter shows both total characters and characters excluding spaces.

Can I use this word counter for assignments?

Yes. It can be used to quickly check the length of essays, assignments, reports and other written work.

What is estimated reading time?

Estimated reading time is an approximate indication of how long it may take to read the text at an average reading speed.
