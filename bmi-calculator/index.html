What is BMI?

BMI stands for Body Mass Index. It is a commonly used screening measurement based on a person's weight and height. A BMI calculator provides an estimate of BMI using these two values.

How is BMI Calculated?

BMI is calculated using the following formula:

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height² (m²)

For example, if a person weighs 70 kg and is 1.75 metres tall:

BMI = 70 ÷ (1.75 × 1.75) = 22.86

BMI Categories

  • Below 18.5 – Underweight
  • 18.5 to 24.9 – Normal weight
  • 25 to 29.9 – Overweight
  • 30 or above – Obesity

These categories are general adult screening ranges. BMI does not directly measure body fat and may not be appropriate for everyone, including children and teenagers.

Why Use a BMI Calculator?

A BMI calculator provides a quick way to estimate BMI from height and weight. It can be useful for general health and fitness tracking.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does BMI stand for?

BMI stands for Body Mass Index.

What information is needed to calculate BMI?

You need your weight in kilograms and height in centimetres or metres.

Is BMI a perfect measure of health?

No. BMI is a screening measure and does not account for factors such as muscle mass, body composition or individual health conditions.

Can students use this BMI calculator?

Adults can use this calculator for a general BMI estimate. For children and teenagers, BMI is interpreted differently based on age and sex, so a healthcare professional should be consulted.
