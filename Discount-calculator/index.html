What is a Discount Calculator?

A discount calculator is a simple online tool that helps you calculate how much money you save when a discount is applied to an original price. It also shows the final price you need to pay after the discount.

How to Calculate Discount?

The discount amount can be calculated using this formula:

Discount Amount = Original Price × Discount Percentage ÷ 100

The final price is:

Final Price = Original Price − Discount Amount

Example

Suppose a product costs ₹2,000 and has a 20% discount.

  • Original Price = ₹2,000
  • Discount = 20%
  • Discount Amount = ₹400
  • Final Price = ₹1,600
  • Total Savings = ₹400

Why Use a Discount Calculator?

A discount calculator makes it easier to compare prices during online shopping, sales and special offers. Students can also use it to calculate savings on books, courses, electronics, subscriptions and other products.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate 20% off a price?

Multiply the original price by 20 and divide by 100. Subtract the resulting discount amount from the original price to get the final price.

What is the formula for discount?

Discount Amount = Original Price × Discount Percentage ÷ 100.

Does the calculator show how much I save?

Yes. The discount amount represents the total amount you save.
