What is a Percentage?

A percentage is a way of expressing a number as a fraction of 100. The word percentage means "per hundred".

Percentages are commonly used in school, college, exams, discounts, marks, statistics and everyday calculations.

How to Calculate a Percentage

To calculate what percentage one number is of another number, use this formula:

Percentage = (Part ÷ Total) × 100

For example, if you scored 45 marks out of 60:

(45 ÷ 60) × 100 = 75%

Therefore, your score is 75%.

Percentage Examples

Example 1: Find 20% of 200

20 ÷ 100 × 200 = 40

Example 2: Exam Percentage

If a student scores 420 marks out of 500:

(420 ÷ 500) × 100 = 84%

Example 3: Percentage Increase

If a price increases from 100 to 120, the increase is 20%.
