What is an Age Calculator?

An age calculator is an online tool that calculates a person's age from their date of birth.

Instead of manually counting years, months and days, you can enter your date of birth and get your age instantly.

How Does an Age Calculator Work?

The calculator compares your date of birth with the current date and determines the difference between the two dates.

The result is displayed as completed years, months and days.

Age = Current Date − Date of Birth

Example of Age Calculation

Suppose a person was born on 15 January 2005. The calculator compares this date with today's date and calculates the completed years, months and days.

This makes it easier to find an exact age without manually calculating the difference between dates.

Why Use an Age Calculator?

An age calculator can be useful when you need to quickly calculate your age for forms, applications, school records, exams or general information.

It can also help when you need to know your age precisely in years, months and days.
