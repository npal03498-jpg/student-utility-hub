What is a QR Code?

A QR code, or Quick Response code, is a two-dimensional barcode that can store information such as website URLs, text and other data. QR codes can be scanned using a smartphone camera or compatible QR scanning application.

How to Create a QR Code?

  1. Enter your text or URL in the box above.
  2. Click the Generate QR Code button.
  3. Your QR code will appear instantly.
  4. Use the Download button to save the QR code.

What Can You Use a QR Code For?

  • Sharing website URLs.
  • Sharing online resources with students.
  • Linking to documents or web pages.
  • Sharing text or simple information.
  • Creating quick access links for projects.

QR Code Generator for Students

Students can use QR codes for presentations, projects, assignments and classroom activities. A QR code can provide a quick way for others to open a website or access shared online information.

QR Codes for Websites

To create a QR code for a website, simply enter the complete website address, including the https:// part when applicable. The generated QR code can then be scanned to open the website.
